Polycentric development narrows regional disparities but does not simultaneously raise economic productivity: the gains of the peripheries are cancelled out by the losses of the urban cores.

Published in *Environment and Planning A* (2023). See the [paper](/research/2015-10-01-paper-title-number-3/).
