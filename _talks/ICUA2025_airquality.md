Does a polycentric spatial structure help clean up a region's air? Using satellite-derived PM2.5 across 50 German urban regions and a multi-scale research design, this paper finds no significant effect on regional air pollution overall, but measurably higher pollution in the urban cores of more polycentric regions.

Published in *Cities* (2026). See the [paper](/research/2026-01-15-paper-title-number-7/).
