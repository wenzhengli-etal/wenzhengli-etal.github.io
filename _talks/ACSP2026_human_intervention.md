Large language models can read public comments at scale, but a credible result still takes human work — and it is rarely clear how much, or where it pays off. Using the public record of 350 Vancouver rezoning projects, this paper prices each stage of human input against the accuracy it buys, across topic classification, sentiment, and named entity recognition.
