I teach quantitative and spatial analysis in planning — statistics, GIS, urban data science, and critical cartography — at both the undergraduate and graduate level. My approach pairs methods with the planning questions that motivate them, and increasingly brings emerging tools (GeoAI, remote sensing, machine learning, LLMs) into the classroom.
